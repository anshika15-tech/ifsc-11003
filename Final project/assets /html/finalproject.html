The Series

The Four Books

Twisted Love

Ava and Alex. A grumpy, guarded billionaire and his best friend's younger sister. Tropes: grumpy/sunshine, brother's best friend.

Twisted Games

Bridget and Rhys. A princess and her bodyguard who aren't supposed to fall for each other. Tropes: forbidden romance, bodyguard.

Twisted Hate

Jules and Josh. Two people who can't stand each other, until they can't stay away. Tropes: enemies to lovers.

Twisted Lies

Stella and Christian. Secrets, tension, and a romance full of complications. Add the main trope from your favorite part of the book.

Why Fans Love Twisted

  • Every book stars a different couple, but they all belong to the same friend group.
  • Favorite characters keep showing up in the other books.
  • It mixes romance, drama, and wealthy, high-stakes settings.
  • Each book uses a popular trope, so it's easy to pick one by mood.

Reading Order

You can read them in this order: Twisted Love, Twisted Games, Twisted Hate, Twisted Lies. Each is a standalone love story, but reading them in order makes the connections clearer.

Content Note

These books are written for adult readers and include mature themes. This site is for fans, so please read at your own comfort level.

The Twisted series follows four connected friends and families through love, secrets, and rivalry. It includes Twisted Love, Twisted Games, Twisted Hate, and Twisted Lies.
